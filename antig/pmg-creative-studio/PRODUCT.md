# Creative Alli Studio

**Users:** PMG media strategists and account managers (non-designers). They use Alli daily for campaign reporting. Comfortable with spreadsheets and the Alli dashboard, but not technical. They're the people who previously handed work to a CRM team bottleneck.

**Product Purpose:** A template-based ad creative builder that eliminates the manual CRM handoff. Users pick an HTML ad template, map their product feed columns to template zones, preview the live result, and batch-generate ad creatives at scale. The wizard guides: pick template → map fields → preview → generate.

**Brand:** Alli by PMG. Professional, data-driven, clean. Not flashy. The design system uses Inter, blue-600 (`#0C69EA`) as brand accent, blue-gray family for neutrals, restrained light theme throughout.

**Register:** product

**Tone:** Confident, concise, helpful. Like a smart coworker, not a marketing voice.

**Anti-references:** Canva (too playful), Google Ads UI (too dense/gray), Figma (too technical). The surface should feel like Alli — familiar to someone already in the Alli 360 dashboard.

**Strategic principles:**
1. Non-creatives should feel capable, not lost. Every step needs a clear path forward.
2. The feed/zone mapping step is the hardest mental model — make it visual and forgiving.
3. Speed matters. Users map 5–15 fields for 100s of rows. Friction is wasted time.
4. Alli brand consistency. Colors, typography, and component patterns from the Alli design system.
